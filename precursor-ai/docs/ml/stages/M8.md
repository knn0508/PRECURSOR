# ML stage M8: Evaluation, calibration, uncertainty and explainability

Status: **NOT_STARTED**. Track: [ADR-0009](../../adr/ADR-0009-ml-development-track.md); status table: [ML_STATUS](../ML_STATUS.md). Executable brief: [Prompts.md](../../../Prompts.md) (section M8). Separate from the 10 roadmap phases; roadmap phases stay ML-free.

## Objective
Evaluation, calibration, uncertainty and explainability. See scope below.

## Scope
- One-shot evaluation on untouched temporal and spatial hold-outs: PR-AUC, ROC-AUC, recall at fixed false-alarm rate, precision at fixed recall, F-beta, Brier, log loss, calibration curve, ECE; trigger task also hit rate, false alarms per area per year, lead time; block-bootstrap confidence intervals.
- Calibration (Platt/isotonic on a separate split) and uncertainty (ensemble variance or conformal, with coverage check).
- Explainability: SHAP, permutation importance, partial dependence, fold stability, plausibility and spurious-feature checks (explanations are not causality).
- Failure and robustness analysis by region, terrain, land cover, rainfall regime, event size, accuracy class; label-noise and background-ratio sensitivity; ablations (no InSAR, no rainfall, static-only).
- Honest conclusion on usefulness versus baselines and conditions of non-use; `ml/reports/M8_evaluation.md`; ML subsection in SCIENTIFIC_LIMITATIONS.

## Out of scope
- Re-tuning after seeing hold-out results, calling outputs probabilities without independent calibration, claiming operational warning capability.
